Questions before we start.

If your question is not here, message me on WhatsApp. I answer personally.

Do I need to change CRMs?

No. The method runs inside the CRM you already use — Salesforce, HubSpot or a spreadsheet. Gina adapts to what exists, not the other way around.

Does it work for a small pet shop?

Yes. The more repeat business you have, the more sense it makes. Pet shops, clinics, salons, gyms and supplement stores are good examples.

Do messages go out automatically?

No. Every send goes through your approval. Gina prepares the task and explains why; you talk to the customer.

What if I have no recorded consent?

Gina blocks contact and shows what is missing. In the diagnostic we organize the proof of consent (date and accepted text) before any campaign.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the base and the scope. After the diagnostic you receive a proposal with a fixed value. No fine print.
