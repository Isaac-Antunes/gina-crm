Method

Simple rules. No black box.

Every customer lands in a lifecycle stage based on a few clear rules. You can read them, get them and tweak them.

New1st order

First purchase in the last 30 days. Gets a nudge toward order number two.

Missed1.5×

1.5× past their usual gap. Gets a low-key reminder on their own schedule.

Dormant3×

3× past their usual gap. Goes on a 90-day cool-off with no automated messages.

VIPTop tier

Top third for visits and spend. Personal outreach only, never automated.

Order of decisions

When two rules apply, the first one on this list wins. Consent comes before everything, VIPs included. Then Dormant, VIP, Missed and New. And nothing goes out without your approval.
