A ready-to-go CRM assistant for small shops. Gina learns how each customer shops, gives the owner a heads-up when someone's drifting and writes the message that brings them back. She plugs right into the CRM the shop already uses.

Meet
Gina

Gina is a CRM assistant with a face. She lives in the shop's messages: the push that pops up at 7 p.m., the email with your favorite product, the WhatsApp text that checks in.

Every shop gives her its own name. Customers meet her in a text and recognize her at the counter.
